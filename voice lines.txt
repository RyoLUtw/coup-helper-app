GENERAL CHALLENGE LINES

These can be used when challenging any character claim.

“I challenge that claim.”

“I doubt you have that influence.”

“Your story doesn’t sound convincing.”

“I need evidence before I believe you.”

“That claim seems suspicious.”

“I’m willing to take the risk. Challenge!”

GENERAL SUCCESSFUL-DEFENSE LINES

These can be used after a player reveals that their challenged claim was true.

“Your assumption was incorrect.”

“Perhaps you judged too quickly.”

“Next time, gather more evidence.”

“Confidence is not the same as accuracy.”

GENERAL VOCABULARY

claim
doubt
convincing
evidence
suspicious
risk
assumption

accuracy
